# SPEAR Master Sales Packages

This document defines the sales and licensing strategy for the **SPEAR (Smart Platform for Every Accommodation & Restaurant)** ecosystem. Because the system is built on a modular Hub-and-Spoke architecture, we do not force clients to purchase the entire ecosystem. Instead, we offer three distinct, highly customizable sales packages tailored to the client's operational needs.

All packages include access to the **Core Hub** (Billing, Events, Analytics, Tenant Management) by default. The specific modules activated for the tenant depend on the package purchased.

---

## 1. The F&B Package
**Target Audience:** Independent restaurants, ghost kitchens, bars, and cafes.

This package provides everything necessary to run a Food & Beverage operation. It entirely excludes accommodation-specific modules (like the PMS and Room Inventory), ensuring the client is not overwhelmed by irrelevant features.

**Included Modules:**
- `pos-system` (Point of Sale)
- `menu-ordering` (QR Codes, Waiter Tablets)
- `digital-menus` (Menu & Recipe Blueprints)
- `kds` (Kitchen Display System)
- `kitchen-inventory` (Stock & COGS Management)
- `restaurant-reservations` (Table Bookings)
- `guest-crm` & `guest-messaging` (F&B configurations)
- `staff-management` (Shift & Timeclock)

*Note: Specific features within these modules can be toggled on/off depending on the client's subscription tier.*

---

## 2. The Accommodation Package
**Target Audience:** Boutique hotels, motels, and hostels without on-site restaurants.

This package provides a pure lodging management system. It strips out all Food & Beverage logic, focusing entirely on room bookings, guest stays, and property maintenance.

**Included Modules:**
- `hotel-pms` (Property Management System - The Master Record)
- `booking-engine` (Direct Web Bookings)
- `rms` (Revenue Management & Dynamic Pricing)
- `mobile-checkin` (Frictionless Arrival & Upsells)
- `smart-locks` (Digital Keys via Apple/Google Wallet)
- `property-inventory` (Housekeeping & Asset Tracking)
- `guest-crm` & `guest-messaging` (Accommodation configurations)
- `staff-management` (Shift & Timeclock)
- `website-builder` & `tenant-websites` (If they require a web presence)

---

## 3. The All-in-One Package (Premium Tier)
**Target Audience:** Full-service hotels, resorts, and large hospitality groups.

This is the complete SPEAR ecosystem. It combines both the F&B and Accommodation packages into a single, unified operational powerhouse. This package unlocks the most advanced cross-module integrations (e.g., charging a restaurant meal directly to a hotel room folio, or booking a banqueting event that requires both hotel rooms and catering).

**Included Modules:**
- **All modules** across the entire ecosystem.
- Exclusive access to cross-functional orchestration modules like `sales-banqueting` (which requires both F&B and PMS capabilities to function correctly).
