Why We Built SPEAR:
Ending the 5-Vendor Operational Nightmare.

For decades, prestigious boutique hotels and luxury estates were forced to stitch together a disparate patchwork of systems: an outdated PMS for rooms, a separate cloud POS for the restaurant, third-party channel managers for OTAs, an independent table booking widget, and manual spreadsheet accounting.

When the internet dropped, dining rooms went dark and servers couldn't print tickets. When a guest at dinner wanted their $300 bill transferred to Room 304, reception had to manually re-enter receipts into a midnight ledger. SPEAR was engineered from the ground up on modern native architectures to eliminate this friction forever.
